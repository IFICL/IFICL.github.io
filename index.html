About

I am a Research Scientist Lead at Luma AI, working on multimodal foundation models.
I obtained my Ph.D. in Computer Vision at the University of Michigan, where I was advised by Prof. Andrew Owens. Prior to that, I earned my Master's degree at the University of Michigan and my Bachelor's degree at Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

My primary research interest lies in Multimodal learning and Computer Vision. During my Ph.D. I interned at Codec Avatars Lab of Meta and SODA (Sound Design AI) Group of Adobe Research. I was fortunate to collaborate with Prof. David Fouhey and Prof. Alex Wong.
